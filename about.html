About Me

A journey of growth, learning, and self-discovery in the HNG program.

My Bio

Hello! I'm Hammed Adedeji (HammedT19), a passionate frontend developer with years of experience building responsive web applications. I'm currently participating in the HNG internship program and I'm excited to learn and grow with the community.

My journey in web development has been driven by a love for creating user-friendly interfaces and solving complex problems through code. I enjoy reading tech blogs and books, working on coding side projects, and staying up-to-date with the latest web technologies.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me watching movies and anime, riding motorcycles, playing chess, or enjoying music. I believe in continuous learning and always strive to improve my skills while contributing to meaningful projects.

Goals in This Program

  • Master Advanced Web Technologies: Deepen my understanding of modern frameworks, APIs, and development practices.
  • Build Real-World Projects: Create meaningful applications that solve actual problems and showcase my capabilities.
  • Improve Collaboration Skills: Work effectively in teams, contribute to open-source projects, and learn from peers.
  • Enhance Problem-Solving: Develop stronger analytical thinking and debugging skills through challenging assignments.
  • Network and Connect: Build relationships with fellow developers, mentors, and industry professionals.
  • Prepare for Career Growth: Gain the skills and confidence needed to advance in my development career.

Areas of Low Confidence

While I'm excited about this journey, I acknowledge there are areas where I'd like to build more confidence:

  • System Design: Understanding how to architect large-scale applications and make informed decisions about technology choices.
  • Performance Optimization: Identifying bottlenecks and implementing efficient solutions for better user experiences.
  • Testing Strategies: Writing comprehensive tests and implementing robust testing practices in my projects.
  • DevOps and Deployment: Managing deployment pipelines, monitoring, and maintaining production applications.
  • Leadership Skills: Taking initiative in projects and guiding others through technical challenges.

I see these as opportunities for growth rather than limitations, and I'm committed to addressing each one throughout this program.
